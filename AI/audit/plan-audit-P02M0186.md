AUDITOR'S REVIEW OF PLAN P02M0186 (2026-09-21T14:11:26Z):

**Rating: 3/10.** The service boundary is appropriate, but commit/lifetime semantics, backpressure, achievable status and the driver integration contract need material correction before implementation.

Reviewed [the plan](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md), its [originating printer requirement](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0099.md:5728), current channel, console and storage contracts, provider discovery, service deployment and guest fixtures at commit `467f761df5a27b5409ffa3cd54e942485b4cf078`. The document is a requirements checklist; the findings concern unresolved implementation decisions and incompatible guarantees, not the expected absence of new code.

1. **High - “Closing it commits” does not distinguish intentional submission from owner death, or preserve the capability needed to observe completion.**

   The [first item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md:21) says closing the job capability commits, while the [lifetime item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md:30) leaves owner-exit behavior undecided and the [definition of done](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md:41) requires reading job status through an outcome. If “close” means releasing the channel handle, normal closure and process death are indistinguishable at the service boundary, and the client has also lost its stated status path. If it means a typed finish operation, that distinction and the surviving status path must be part of the contract.

   The current runtime reports a closed client as [`DISCONNECT_OP`](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/LSIDL.md:443); channel closure is not evidence of successful completion. Existing transactional storage instead has [explicit commit and abort operations](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/storage.lsidl:346) and specifies [uncommitted channel closure as abort](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/storage.lsidl:283). Leaving this ambiguity can submit a crashed application's partial document or make the job's outcome unobservable.

   **Correct the first interface item, lifetime item and definition of done** with an explicit state machine distinguishing writing, submitted/queued, active and terminal jobs. Define a typed submit/finish operation separately from handle release or disconnect, and retain either the job channel for status or a separately scoped status capability. Decide owner-exit behavior before and after submission, release of quota, and service-restart behavior. Test a crash mid-write, a normal submission followed by status observation, repeated submission and disconnect after submission. These decisions do not require durable spooling unless durability is explicitly chosen.

2. **High - Commit-on-close and device-paced backpressure are incompatible without a staging and progress model.**

   The [append/commit item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md:21) implies that a job is staged before submission. The [backpressure item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md:23) instead defines application writes in terms of whether the printer has taken the bytes. It does not identify the bounded staging store, when printing may start, or what event makes a blocked pre-commit write writable again.

   Under a literal implementation, a job can fill its staging capacity before the client reaches close; returning `again` until the printer drains it makes no progress because the uncommitted job cannot yet print. Sending bytes immediately avoids that deadlock but makes printing occur before the stated commit point. The referenced [console write contract](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/device.lsidl:371) reports an accepted byte count and distinguishes `again` from a removed port; it does not promise future acceptance regardless of removal. The common [error vocabulary](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/base.lsidl:84) also distinguishes transient blocking from exhausted resources.

   **Correct the append, backpressure and quota items** to choose bounded staging followed by submission, or an explicitly different streaming commitment model. Specify what a successful application write accepts, whether a prefix can be accepted, the maximum write frame, when bytes become eligible for backend transmission, and the exact progress condition for `again`. A job exceeding its hard cap needs a terminal size/quota refusal, not endless retries; aggregate queued bytes and job admission must remain bounded too. If staging is in RAM, select a feasible maximum accepted job size; a persistent spool is not inherently required. Test a job larger than the transient buffer, capacity exhaustion before commit, a stalled backend and partial acceptance without duplicate bytes.

3. **High - The required status precision exceeds the standard printer transport's available evidence.**

   The [status item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md:27) lists cover-open and jammed alongside paper and offline states as unconditional outcomes. The originating scope begins with standard bulk transport, port status and reset. Standard `GET_PORT_STATUS` exposes paper-empty, selected and generic error bits, not separate cover-open or jam codes; devices unable to determine status may report benign values. Richer protocol-specific status is a separate mechanism. [USB Printer Class 1.1 specification, sections 3.3 and 4.2.2](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/usbprint11a021811.pdf#page=10).

   A host status decoder therefore cannot truthfully derive every required state from the baseline backend. The plan also leaves “outcome” undefined: accepted bytes and a benign port status do not by themselves establish that a physical page finished. An unplug after some data was transmitted can leave output partial or uncertain. Inferring a more precise result produces false status; adding enough printer-specific protocols solely to satisfy this list would expand the deliberately narrow scope.

   **Correct the status item and terminal-state tests** to separate transport/device observations from job state and from optional detailed printer status. Define generic-error, unavailable/unknown detail and unsupported detail where the backend cannot distinguish causes. Allow jam/cover details only when an identified backend supplies them. State whether baseline success means all bytes were transferred, and reserve physical-print completion for a backend that can establish it. Preserve unplug as a distinct cause while recording possible partial/uncertain delivery; do not silently replay such a job onto a replacement device. Test the limited baseline status as well as optional detailed observations.

4. **Medium - “Already-rendered languages” has no format selection or device-capability contract behind its typed refusal.**

   The [language item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md:32) says to refuse anything else, but the described job API takes only appended bytes and does not identify a declared document language, the first supported language set, or where a device's supported languages come from. Rendered output for one printer language is not automatically usable by a different printer. USB Printer Class provides language/capability information through `GET_DEVICE_ID`, separately from port status. [USB Printer Class 1.1 specification, sections 3.3.2 and 4.2.1](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/usbprint11a021811.pdf#page=12).

   Without this decision, the implementation can either accept an incompatible rendered document, reject every useful job, or invent content sniffing/rendering that the milestone deliberately excludes.

   **Correct the device and job-creation contract** to carry a bounded declared language/format and the backend's supported-language information, with an explicit initial support policy. Define typed rejection before transmission for an unsupported language and behavior when capability information is absent or malformed. Test supported, unsupported and unknown device-language combinations. Checking declared format against supported capabilities is sufficient for this boundary; a renderer or a general document-language parser is not required.

5. **High - The private backend contract and executable service proof are missing, leaving the prerequisite dependent on the driver it is meant to unblock.**

   The [definition of done](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md:41) requires a useful job capability, and the [footer](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md:46) says this unblocks the printer driver. Yet the [driver item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0099.md:5728) still owns transport, port status and reset. The plan gives no backend protocol for these operations, no device discovery/withdrawal path, and no service registration or client grant path. The closed [provider vocabulary](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/device.lsidl:151) and [manifest kinds](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/tools/system-manifest/src/lib.rs:166) have no printer provider today. Reusing console write's result shape does not provide a printer lifecycle or status backend.

   The [guest-gate item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0186.md:36) also selects no data sink or status/unplug injector; the [existing USB gadget helper](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/harness/usb-gadget.sh:73) has no printer kind. An online service or returned job handle would not prove bytes reached a sink, jobs did not interleave, or quota/status behavior worked.

   **Correct the interface and integration/test items** to specify the bounded private backend contract, its sole service consumer, provider identity/generation, attach/removal and reset/failure behavior, and how one active job serializes each device. Name the service binary, [deployment and bootstrap roles](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/services/manifest.toml:2443), generated IDL/client bindings and the trusted path that grants applications only spool/job authority. Keep USB binding in its owning driver work. Choose a service-side fixture that records bytes and injects capacity, status and removal, so the prerequisite closes independently; state that it is not USB transport coverage. Put the production job state machine in a [host-testable leaf](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/services/logic/Cargo.toml:8), and require a real guest client to verify two-job serialization, committed bytes, backpressure, owner exit and final status. Register the gate with concrete [coverage and prerequisites](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/tools/verify-model/src/catalog.rs:892).
