1. **High - Every measured interval contains a pacing sleep of at least 16 ms, rounded up to the 10 ms scheduler tick, and the plan presents that time as unexplained present-path cost without identifying or classifying it.**

   The plan opens on the 52.8 ms by which the demo's present interval exceeds its draw. It describes that time as spent ["between the renderer handing over a finished surface and the same loop being free to draw the next one"](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:8). DisplayService completes every present [with no timing at all](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/services/core/src/display_service.rs:818). So when a present returns, `Pacing::on_present` schedules the next frame at [now plus `UNPACED_INTERVAL_NS`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/libs/graphics/app/src/pacing.rs:168), which is [16 ms](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/libs/graphics/app/src/pacing.rs:92). The time is stamped [after the call returns](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/libs/graphics/app/src/frame_loop.rs:140).

   The next step answers [`Idle` until then](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/libs/graphics/app/src/pacing.rs:234) and the demo [parks](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/apps/tools/src/test2d_sw.rs:710). `park` converts the deadline to an absolute tick and [rounds it up](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/libs/graphics/app/src/frame_loop.rs:193), because [every ABI deadline is on the 100 Hz tick counter](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/abi/src/lib.rs:221). A wait expires only when [`ticks() >= deadline`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/kernel/syscall/mod.rs:3697).

   By construction, then, each frame of [the demo's interval](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/apps/tools/src/test2d_sw.rs:649) contains an idle of at least 16 ms, and up to about 26 ms once rounded. That is a fallback constant plus tick quantisation, not transport, service or driver work. The plan's last term, "what the application then waits for", would capture how long the idle lasts, but nothing in the plan identifies what it is.

   The instrument item asks only for [the resolution of the timestamp clock](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:63). The primitives measure only [a channel wake](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:94). [The four-way classification](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:124) has no class for a deliberate policy wait. The idle will therefore be reported as "a scheduling consequence" or folded into ["what this system charges for being a system"](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:110). That puts a frame-loop fallback at the centre of the milestone's headline number, and it aims a new cross-process instrument at a term the source code already states.

   **Correct the opening, present-path, instrument and verdict items.** Name the frame loop's pacing wait as its own term: the fallback interval applied because the backend reports no timing, together with its tick rounding. For every wait, record whether it ended on a message or on a deadline, with the deadline requested and the time the thread actually ran. Measure a timer-deadline wake beside the channel wake. Give the classification a class for policy waits and tick quantisation, or state that such time is reported but excluded from the system's charge. Measuring the wait is enough; changing the pacing stays out of scope.

2. **High - The plan models the display half as moving a 640x480 frame to the device, but DisplayService scales every surface to the fixed scanout and the driver's call includes the device's own completion, so the copy count and the two-size item will misattribute the display terms.**

[The size item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:85) treats a term that does not move with the frame's area as "not paying for the picture".

   `pix::blit` copies rows only when [the surface and scanout are the same size](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/libs/image/pix/src/lib.rs:445). Otherwise it [fits the surface to the whole scanout, pixel by pixel](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/libs/image/pix/src/lib.rs:459), and DisplayService always [blits into the full scanout](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/services/core/src/display_service.rs:1178). The live harness boots [`virtio-vga` with no resolution set](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/harness/qemu-run.sh:2240), and QEMU's defaults are `xres=1280`, `yres=800` (from `qemu-system-x86_64 -device virtio-vga,help`).

   At [the baseline's `--size=640x480`](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/PERF.md:9), each whole-surface present therefore writes a 1066x800 output, about 2.8 times the surface's pixels, and the driver transfers that rectangle. Both are set by the scanout, not by the frame. The tree already reads [a present that stays flat across three surface sizes](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/PERF.md:316) as the display's own pacing.

   The driver also answers only after the device has [acknowledged both commands](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/display-device.lsidl:85), [the transfer and the flush](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/drivers/core/src/virtio_gpu.rs:259). That device time is work done by QEMU on the host, and its cost depends on how the guest was started: the [display back end](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/harness/qemu-run.sh:109) and the [vCPU count, which defaults to the host's](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/harness/qemu-run.sh:23). It also comes after "the device being told", where [the term list stops](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:72), so it would be absorbed into "what the application then waits for".

   The consequences are threefold. Measured at two surface sizes on a fixed scanout, the blit and the transfer will be flat, and will be reported as exactly the non-picture cost the milestone is looking for. The copy count will describe a copy that does not happen in that form. The driver term will be neither reproducible nor classifiable as work or wait.

   **Correct the copy-count, two-size and present-path items.** Record the scanout size, the direct or scaled path, and the source and output pixels of every run; DisplayService [already counts all of these](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/display.lsidl:265). Vary the scanout and the surface together, either through the device's `xres`/`yres` or by running the surface at the screen's own size, so that the direct path is what gets measured. Name the device's acknowledgement (from told to acknowledged, as the driver sees it) as a term. Record QEMU's display device, display back end and vCPU count as measurement conditions. State how a spin-wait on the device is split between work and wait.

5. **Medium - Every term must be a work/wait pair, but the specified instrument records only user-side timestamps and the kernel does not account run time, so preemption inside a term cannot be told apart from work.**

   The plan reports [every term as a pair](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:79), and [the gate fails](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:150) when a pair is missing. The instrument, however, is a per-process ring of [(site, timestamp) pairs](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:52). Timestamps do show a cross-process boundary's wait, as the gap between two sites. But a thread preempted inside a term looks identical to one that is computing.

   The scheduler is [round robin with a one-tick quantum](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/kernel/sched/mod.rs:4), and it puts a woken thread on [the waker's run queue](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/kernel/sched/mod.rs:12). Nothing reports on-CPU time: [`ProcessStats`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/abi/src/lib.rs:1012) carries messages, handles, memory and state only.

   The distinction the item calls "opposite findings with opposite fixes" is therefore not measurable as specified. The implementer must either add kernel scheduler accounting that the plan does not scope, or report a "work" figure that silently includes run-queue time, which the gate would accept.

   **Correct the instrument and wait/work items.** Either add a per-thread on-CPU time counter (or switch records on the same clock) that the instrument samples at each site, as the kernel change [the exception clause](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:138) admits, and define wait as blocked time plus time spent runnable but not running. Or narrow the pair explicitly to time blocked in a syscall versus everything else, and state that preemption is not separated.

6. **Medium - The instrument is designed from scratch around a frame identity the application allocates and the path carries, which duplicates the tree's existing cross-process tracing and counters and would change the display wire protocol the plan must not touch.**

   The tree already has a switchable cross-process marker facility, made of these parts:
   - [`rt::PERF`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/runtime/rt/src/lib.rs:584) compiles markers out.
   - [`perf_now`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/runtime/rt/src/lib.rs:599) reads the TSC in ring 3, without a syscall.
   - [`perf_mark`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/runtime/rt/src/lib.rs:636) emits markers to the debug serial.
   - The kernel publishes the TSC rate only under [the `development-trace` boot profile](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/kernel/main.rs:442), which any process can read with [`rt::boot_profile`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/runtime/rt/src/lib.rs:2403).
   - [`perf-trace.py`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/harness/perf-trace.py:1) places every process's markers on one timeline, with [a phase breakdown down to the GPU present](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/harness/perf-trace.py:257).
   - [The `perf-anchor` gate](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/tools/check-perf-anchor.sh:1) guards it, and [DEBUG.md](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/DEBUG.md:256) documents it.

   DisplayService also already [times its blit and its driver call on every present](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/services/core/src/display_service.rs:759), and serves the totals through [`display-admin.stats`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/display.lsidl:443).

   The plan nevertheless says [no measurement says where the time goes](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:9) and specifies a new facility. It leaves open how that facility's buffers leave DisplayService and the driver, and how [the "runtime request"](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:60) reaches them. Those are exactly the questions that decide whether a new IDL operation, a new grant, or the unprivileged debug-write path is needed.

   The ["frame identity the application allocates and the path carries"](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:54) has nothing to carry it. [`surface.present`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/display.lsidl:366) carries the image, the configuration serial, the generation and the damage; [`display-device.present`](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/display-device.lsidl:88) carries only the generation and the damage. That contradicts the plan's own [compiled-out rule](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:60) and [exception rule](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0189.md:138).

   The field is also unnecessary. A device present is [synchronous, with one in flight per connection](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/display-device.lsidl:32), and DisplayService already allocates [a present serial](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/services/core/src/display_service.rs:724) that the application [receives in the reply](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/services/core/src/display_service.rs:753).

   **Correct the instrument items.** Build on the existing facility: its ring-3 clock and published rate, the boot profile as the runtime request, and the host collector. Replace per-marker serial writes with the fixed per-process buffer, emitted after the measured frames. State how each process's records leave it. Join application and DisplayService records by the existing present serial, and the driver's records by call nesting, with no wire change. Use DisplayService's existing counters as the cross-check for its terms.
