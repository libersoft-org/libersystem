AUDITOR'S REVIEW OF PLAN P02M0184 (2026-09-21T14:10:15Z):

Reviewed [the plan](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0184.md), [the originating UVC requirement](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0099.md:5713), shared-memory and capability mechanisms, current provider/bootstrap architecture and guest verification infrastructure at commit `467f761df5a27b5409ffa3cd54e942485b4cf078`. This document is a requirements checklist; the findings identify missing or incorrect implementation decisions, not the expected absence of camera feature code.

1. **High - Capability-scoped buffers have no ownership, reuse or aggregate accounting contract.**

   The [frame-buffer item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0184.md:21) and [definition of done](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0184.md:41) promise bounded frames without saying who allocates them, when a consumer owns a stable frame, or when the producer may overwrite or reuse the backing. Kernel [memory-object creation charges the creator's domain](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/kernel/syscall/mod.rs:641); [the font contract](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/font.lsidl:100) explicitly documents why moving such handles does not transfer that charge. A frame-size cap therefore does not bound service memory when a client keeps requesting and retaining frames.

   Conversely, recycling a small pool without a defined lease/return rule can overwrite a frame while a client reads it. Reusing a backing for another capture grant while an earlier holder can still map it can disclose later frames. [Memory mappings are tracked in the receiving address space](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/kernel/syscall/mod.rs:2647); changing a service-side sequence number is not a revocation mechanism. Even the existing [display-device contract](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/display-device.lsidl:12) explicitly settles backing ownership, generations and connection-loss behavior.

   **Correct the buffer and negotiated-stream items** to choose an implementable ownership model, such as a bounded per-stream pool with explicit leases or appropriately scoped caller-owned buffers. Define allocation/accounting ownership, maximum frame bytes and outstanding buffers/streams, mapping rights, valid byte length/layout, return/reuse rules, pool exhaustion and slow-client policy. Specify stop, process death, renegotiation and unplug behavior, including what happens to retained mappings before memory is reused across grants. Select an explicit concurrent-open policy so incompatible negotiations cannot silently change another client's stream. Verify retained buffers, exhaustion, teardown and cross-client isolation through the production path.

2. **High - Mandatory device timestamps and sequence gaps overstate what the backend can know.**

   The [timestamp item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0184.md:25) and [completion condition](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0184.md:41) require both timestamps for every frame and imply they establish capture age. UVC's payload timestamp fields are conditional; its PTS is expressed in the device's own clock units. The same payload header identifies frame boundaries with a toggling FID bit, not an absolute sequence counter. Consequently, a missing device timestamp cannot honestly be fabricated, and FID alone cannot recover an exact count of every frame lost before observation. These facts follow from the [USB-IF UVC 1.5 specification, section 2.4.3.3, Tables 2-5 and 2-6, pages 29-32](https://forums.developer.nvidia.com/uploads/short-url/ig1j25sqsBdPWvS8gAD62hF6ysB.pdf#page=43).

   Locally, [TimeService timestamps are wall-clock seconds](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/time.lsidl:7), while [the runtime also supplies monotonic nanoseconds](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/runtime/rt/src/lib.rs:2348). Carrying two unqualified integers does not establish a common epoch or resolution. The [loss item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0184.md:28) also fails to distinguish drops counted by the service from discontinuities whose missing-frame count is unknown.

   **Correct the metadata items and definition of done** to include explicit timestamp validity, clock domain/units and reset/wrap behavior, and define exactly which arrival event is measured in the host monotonic domain. Preserve a device timestamp when available; expose missing or uncorrelated timing honestly. Either provide a bounded clock-correlation rule for capture-age claims or remove those claims when correlation is unavailable. Define when the service sequence advances, preserve gaps for known discarded frames, and expose unknown discontinuities separately. Test absent timestamps, device reset/wrap, host/device clock mismatch and losses before and after frame assembly.

3. **High - The camera permission has no minting/enforcement path or termination semantics.**

   The [capability item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0184.md:30) correctly rejects starting capture merely by connecting, but never defines the endpoint that authorizes a specific camera, who can issue it, or how that endpoint differs from enumeration access. There is no camera capability in the current [security vocabulary](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/security.lsidl:12). The existing capture precedent uses [a privileged audio factory](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/audio.lsidl:42), [a launch-scoped PermissionManager grant](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/services/core/src/permission_manager.rs:583), and [an admin endpoint supplied by ServiceManager](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/services/core/src/service_manager/bootstrap.rs:1269).

   A new package plus an `open` operation is insufficient to establish the promised separation: implementation could expose the start method on every ordinary connection or leave capture running after the holder exits. Referring to a future portal supplies neither the current issuer nor the cleanup behavior.

   **Correct the camera-authority item and implementation scope** to specify enumeration access, scoped capture authority, the trusted minting endpoint and the explicit start operation. Identify bootstrap/deployment, generated protocol/client and permission-policy integration, using the existing factory pattern where appropriate. Define device identity/generation checks and what close, loss of authority, client death and device removal do to active capture and its buffers. Require negative guest tests proving that ordinary service access cannot start capture, alongside a permitted client that explicitly starts and stops it. A portal UI need not be implemented here.

4. **High - The service-before-driver ordering has no concrete provider boundary or runnable camera fixture.**

   The [introduction](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0184.md:11) and [footer](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0184.md:47) put the vocabulary/service before UVC, but the [test items](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0184.md:35) already require malformed descriptor handling and a guest gate over an emulated camera. The [waiting UVC item](/data/yellow/libersystem/docs/todo/P02M0099.md:5713) separately owns descriptor parsing, probe/commit negotiation and payload assembly. Current [USB class dispatch](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/drivers/core/src/xhci.rs:1147) has no camera path, the [provider-kind set](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/device.lsidl:151) has no camera provider, and neither [current QEMU USB attachment](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/harness/qemu-run.sh:868) nor [the gadget helper's supported kinds](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/harness/usb-gadget.sh:73) supplies the stated fixture.

   This leaves a completion dependency on the driver that is itself waiting for this milestone, unless implementation invents an unstated substitute. It also leaves two potential owners for format parsing/negotiation and no agreed provider contract for transporting frames, limits and events. A fixture returning hard-coded public replies would not validate production negotiation or buffer behavior.

   **Correct the ordering, interface and test items together** to specify a bounded driver-facing contract and assign descriptor parsing, hardware negotiation, frame assembly and service policy to their production owners. Include provider publication/discovery and withdrawal using the [existing catalogue connection model](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/idl/device.lsidl:309). Select the supported initial frame formats and metadata needed by their consumers, including the compressed/uncompressed distinction, payload lengths and memory layout. Choose either a service-level emulated provider exercising that same contract or an explicitly coordinated UVC/backend slice, and state the transport coverage actually obtained. Define guest assertions for negotiated parameters, known frame contents, buffer bounds, authority and loss/timing metadata; register the scenario, relevant component coverage and image/fixture prerequisites in the [verification model](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/tools/verify-model/src/catalog.rs:892). Keep pure enumeration/negotiation logic host-testable under the [existing runtime-free leaf convention](/data/yellow/libersystem/src/user/services/logic/Cargo.toml:8).
