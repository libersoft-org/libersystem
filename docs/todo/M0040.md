# M0040 - ServiceManager: restart policy and watchdog

Status: COMPLETE (2026-06-27).

The supervisor an unattended edge node relies on: a restart policy with backoff and escalation, a
watchdog for **hung** services rather than only crashed ones, reverse-dependency teardown, and a
client-driven stop.

*Condensed 2026-08-08.*

**The machinery is exercised by a canary the supervisor owns.** A managed `watchdog_probe` proves the
live path with a positive heartbeat, then answers a commanded `CRASH` with a ring-3 null write - which
peer-closes its control channel and drives the restart policy - and a commanded `HANG` by parking on
a channel that never becomes ready.

That second one is why the watchdog exists: **a crash announces itself through a peer-close and a
hang announces nothing.** A heartbeat operation answered by the shared serve loop, probed with a
deadline, is what turns silence into an event.

Restart policy is a budget with linear backoff and escalation once it is spent. Crash detection then
applies to every real service in the supervisor's standing wait loop, which also fields the stop path
and the stats query.

**A client-driven `stop <service>` computes the reverse-dependency closure** - a fixpoint over the
manifest - and tears dependents down before their dependencies, **exempting the issuing shell**. That
exemption is not a convenience: without it, stopping something the shell depends on takes the
terminal with it and the operator cannot see what happened.
