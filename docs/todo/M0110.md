# M0110 - Runtime elasticity: NetworkService capacity and device hotplug

Status: COMPLETE. The review's "static at boot" cluster - and both halves turned out to be **mostly
already true**, with observability the real gap.

*Condensed 2026-08-08.*

The client, socket, listener and TCP connection pools already grew on demand, and exhaustion already
returned the typed retry error at the **true** ceiling: channel creation fails when the domain's
handle budget is spent, and the operations map that to the error. The compile-time constants had
stopped being the bound before anyone noticed.

What was missing was that **nobody could see it**. A capacity operation on the network contract
reports the live client, socket and listener counts plus the stack's live connections, and `ss`
prints them as a footer.

USB hotplug was likewise already implemented: the xHCI driver reconciles its ports on a port-status
change event, so a device attached after boot enumerates at runtime.

Worth keeping as a pattern: **a limit that was raised and a limit that was removed look identical
from outside until something reports the utilisation.**
