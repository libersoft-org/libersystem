# P02M0165 - A driver that stops answering looks exactly like one that is busy

Status: PLANNED AFTER P02M0164. SUPERVISION, PLANNED STOP AND ACCOUNTING. SIXTH OF SEVEN - see P02M0098.

## Goal

Notice a driver that has stopped making progress, stop one on purpose without losing its in-flight
work, and keep the resource counters honest across every crash and restart.

## What is there now

- **AN EXIT IS NOTICED AND A HANG IS NOT.** P02M0162 supervises a driver that exits. A driver that is
  alive and wedged - a control queue that never completes, a request outstanding forever - is
  indistinguishable from an idle one, and an idle device is not a fault.
- **THERE IS NO PLANNED STOP.** Shutdown revokes; nothing asks a driver to drain first. Storage
  flushes because `StorageService` flushes, not because the driver was told to.
- **RESOURCES ARE COUNTED PER DOMAIN AND NOT PER BINDING.** A driver's frames, handles, IRQ slots and
  IOMMU mappings are the Domain's; a failed bind returns them because the Domain dies, not because
  anything reconciled them. `dma: DEGRADED ISOLATION` gained a per-device row this round only after a
  boot showed the list keeping a device that had given the bus back.
- **DEVICEMANAGER'S OWN CRASH IS UNHANDLED.** It holds every claim in the system.

## Work

- [ ] **M1 - a heartbeat and a progress deadline, appropriate to the driver.** The protocol gains
  `HEARTBEAT` - here, where there is finally a caller for it. A deadline is declared by the registry
  entry and is about the driver's control path, not about whether its device is busy: a driver may
  not pet its watchdog through an unrelated child.

- [ ] **M2 - a wedged driver is torn down like a crashed one, and its device is quarantined if it
  keeps happening.** The teardown is P02M0162's transaction; what this adds is the reason for
  starting it and a bounded diagnostic capture taken before the process is gone.

- [ ] **M3 - `QUIESCE` and `STOP`.** Quiesce stops accepting new work and drains or explicitly cancels
  what is in flight; storage flushes; `STOP` reaches `STOPPED` before resources are revoked. A
  deadline that expires forces the revocation and says it was forced - never that a clean flush
  happened.

- [ ] **M4 - shutdown and bus removal traverse dependents in reverse.** A controller going away stops
  its children first. The panic path may force revoke and must not claim otherwise.

- [ ] **M5 - every resource is accounted to its binding.** Frames, handles, IPC queues, MMIO windows,
  IRQ slots, DMA-pinned bytes and IOMMU mappings, with per-instance bounds from the registry under
  the system maxima. A failed bind, restart or unplug returns the counters exactly to baseline, and
  exhaustion is a typed bind failure rather than a half-online driver.

- [ ] **M6 - DeviceManager can crash and be rebuilt.** It runs above a driver subtree; if it dies,
  ServiceManager withdraws the provider roots, kills the subtree and starts a cold reconstruction
  with a new epoch. It never inherits a live claim it cannot account for.

- [ ] **M7 - a standing fault-permutation gate.** Crash during bind, crash with DMA in flight,
  simultaneous crash and removal and restart, a stale report after replacement, rapid replug, a
  watchdog expiry, a DeviceManager restart. After every round: at most one claim owner, no stale
  provider, no leaked process, handle, vector, mapping or counter.

## Definition of done

- A driver that stops making progress is detected, torn down and rebound or quarantined, and an idle
  driver is not.
- `QUIESCE` drains or cancels in-flight work and `STOP` reaches `STOPPED` before revocation; a forced
  teardown says it was forced.
- Every resource is charged to a binding, and a failed bind or a restart returns every counter to
  baseline, asserted rather than assumed.
- DeviceManager can be killed and reconstructed without a claim being left owned by nothing.
- The fault-permutation gate is registered and has been watched to fail.

## What the audit found before implementation (2026-08-26)

- **THE HEARTBEAT EXISTS AND ACCEPTS ANYTHING AS A PONG.** `rt::heartbeat` sends the reserved opcode
  and returns true for `Polled::Message { .. }` whatever came back, so a driver answering with
  unrelated traffic reads as responsive. On a driver channel it becomes `PING`/`PONG { generation,
  sequence }`, handled by the same control loop as every other event rather than by a second one.

- **WITHOUT SUSPEND THERE IS NO SECOND VERB.** One `STOP` means: take no new work, finish or
  explicitly abandon what was accepted, flush, call the existing `device_quiesced`, answer `STOPPED`.
  A separate `QUIESCE` followed by `STOP` is two round trips for one transition, and the second one
  has nothing left to do. A timeout is a forced teardown that says it was forced.

- **DO NOT BUILD A SECOND SET OF BOOKS.** One child `Domain` per binding already accounts for memory,
  handles, threads, IPC, DMA and stack, and `DomainStats` already reports them. What is genuinely not
  in it is device-specific: MMIO windows, IRQ vectors, IOMMU grants. That small ledger is the only
  new accounting.

- **M6 DEPENDS ON SOMETHING THAT DOES NOT EXIST YET.** Cold reconstruction has to find the SAME system
  disk again, and today the first virtio-blk driver to finish becomes the system volume - `if
  *block_client == 0`. So a rebuild after a crash can promote a different disk. The system volume is
  named in the boot medium's signed manifest since P02M0150, and the loader reads it from there, so
  the authenticated identity exists; what is missing is that DeviceManager matches a block provider
  against it instead of taking the first. That is one item in this milestone, not a milestone of its
  own - the audit proposed a separate signed-locator milestone against a tree where the pairing was
  still an unsigned file beside the manifest, and it is not one any more.

- **SERVICEMANAGER OWNS DEVICEMANAGER'S `Domain` AND ITS DRIVER SUBTREE.** On a DeviceManager crash it
  withdraws the provider roots, kills that Domain, and only then reconstructs - otherwise the old
  drivers are still holding claims the new manager is about to hand out.

- **"EVERY FAULT PERMUTATION" IS NOT A TEST PLAN.** A named table of the races that are actually
  reachable - a device removed mid-bind, a driver that answers `READY` after its deadline, a crash
  between publish and subscribe - is a plan. A cartesian product is a way of never finishing.

## What this milestone refuses, and why

- **Suspend and resume of the whole machine.** Planned stop is what a driver needs to be taken away
  safely. System power states are a separate subject with their own firmware surface, and nothing
  here needs them.
- **A general watchdog service.** The deadline belongs to the binding, beside the thing it is
  watching.
- **Predicting hardware failure.** Quarantine after a declared number of failed rebinds is a policy
  anyone can read. Anything cleverer is guessing.
