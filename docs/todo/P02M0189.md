# P02M0189 - Where the system's time goes, measured layer by layer

Status: OPEN. It closes when every implementation and verification item below is done.

THE FACT THIS MILESTONE OPENS ON IS ALREADY IN THE TREE, AND NOBODY HAS EXPLAINED IT. The live 2D
demo at 640x480 reports two numbers of its own: a frame's DRAW costs 79.6 ms mean and the loop's
PRESENT INTERVAL comes to 132.4 ms. The difference is 52.8 ms - forty percent of every frame - and
it is spent somewhere between the renderer handing over a finished surface and the same loop being
free to draw the next one. No measurement in this tree says where. `docs/PERF.md` reads that pair as
"the draw is most of the interval, so what the loop waits for is the renderer rather than the
display", which is true of the larger term and says nothing about the other forty percent.

AND THE RENDERER HAS BEEN MEASURED TO DEATH WHILE THAT NUMBER SAT THERE. soft2d has a probe suite
down to the nanosecond per pixel, soft3d has one per stage, and both have found and closed real
defects - `Tile::store`'s per-pixel encode was ninety-one percent of a full-screen fill. What has
never been measured is everything the renderer hands its result TO. So the graphics work has a floor
it can quote and the system around it has none, and a frame that misses its ceiling cannot be
attributed to either.

THIS MILESTONE PRODUCES AN ACCOUNT AND NOT AN OPTIMIZATION. Its product is a breakdown in which every
millisecond of a frame belongs to a named layer, a repeatable instrument that produces it again, and
- where the answer is that this system has no primitive for something and does it the long way
instead - a named follow-up item that says what is missing. Making any layer faster is explicitly
NOT in this milestone, with one exception stated in its own item below. A measurement milestone that
starts fixing things stops measuring, and the thing it was going to fix is usually not the one that
mattered.

## P02M0189 - What it measures, and the order

- [ ] x86_64 FIRST AND ALONE until the account is complete. The two emulated ports charge per
  instruction, so they magnify the terms that execute the most instructions and shrink the ones that
  wait - which is the opposite of the distortion this milestone is looking for. They are measured at
  the end, as a CHECK on the account rather than as a source of it, and a term whose share moves
  between the three is a term the account has to explain.
- [ ] The measured path is one frame, end to end, in a booted guest: an application's draw call, the
  graphics library, the surface it publishes, the transport that carries it, DisplayService, the
  display driver, and the scanout or flush that makes it visible - then back to the application being
  free to draw again. Every boundary crossed is a named term in the breakdown. THE PATH IS THE ONE
  THE DEMOS ALREADY USE and no second path is built to measure it.
- [ ] The primitives under that path are measured separately and in their own item: one IPC round
  trip, one syscall, the wake latency from a channel becoming readable to the waiting thread running,
  one page mapped, one page copied, and the timer resolution every one of those is measured against.
  These are what the per-layer terms are BUILT of, and a layer costing four times its own primitives
  is a different finding from one costing exactly them.
- [ ] THE ACCOUNT MUST ADD UP, and this is the completion criterion that cannot be weakened. The sum
  of the named terms is within five percent of the measured present interval. Unattributed time is
  the defect this milestone exists to remove, so "the rest" is not a term; an interval that will not
  close is reported as an open question with the residue's size, not as a finished account.

## P02M0189 - The instrument

- [ ] Add a timing facility that spans processes, because every interesting boundary here is one.
  It records a bounded number of (site, timestamp) pairs per frame into a fixed-size per-process
  buffer, and the collector joins them by a frame identity the application allocates and the path
  carries. No allocation, no formatting and no channel traffic on the recording side.
- [ ] PROVE THE INSTRUMENT DOES NOT MEASURE ITSELF. Record the same frame with the instrument
  enabled and disabled and report both intervals; the difference is the instrument's cost and it is
  stated in the account beside every number it produced. An instrument costing more than the term it
  is placed around is measuring the instrument, and the way that is noticed is by having the number.
- [ ] It is compiled out by default and turned on by one declared build flag and one runtime
  request, so nothing on the ordinary path pays for it. The flag is named in the account, because a
  measurement nobody can reproduce from the source tree is an anecdote.
- [ ] The timestamps come from one clock with one documented resolution, and the resolution is
  measured rather than assumed. A term of 200 us measured on a clock that ticks every millisecond is
  not a term; it is a rounding.

## P02M0189 - The present path, layer by layer

- [ ] Break the 52.8 ms into named terms: what the application spends after its last draw call and
  before it publishes; what publication itself costs; how long the surface waits before DisplayService
  looks at it; what DisplayService does with it, separated into composition and everything else; what
  the display driver spends between being handed the result and the device being told; and what the
  application then waits for before it may draw again.
- [ ] COUNT THE COPIES OF THE FRAME, which is a number rather than a time and is the first thing a
  reader of this account will want. How many times does a 640x480 frame's pixels move between the
  application's draw target and the scanout memory the device reads, and is each of them necessary?
  A copy nobody can justify is the shape of a missing primitive, and this milestone names it as one
  rather than removing it.
- [ ] MEASURE THE WAIT SEPARATELY FROM THE WORK at every boundary. A layer that takes 20 ms because
  it computed for 20 ms and one that takes 20 ms because it was not scheduled for 19 of them are
  opposite findings with opposite fixes, and a breakdown that reports one number for both cannot tell
  them apart. Every term is reported as a pair.
- [ ] Measure the same path with DisplayService composing ONE surface and with it composing several,
  because a cost that is per-surface and a cost that is per-frame separate only when the count varies.
- [ ] Measure it at two sizes and confirm which terms scale with pixels and which do not. A term that
  does not move with the frame's area is not paying for the picture, and those are the terms this
  milestone is looking for.

## P02M0189 - The primitives underneath

- [ ] Measure one IPC round trip on the transport this path actually uses, at the message sizes it
  actually sends, with the two ends in separate processes and separate Domains - not a loopback in
  one process, which measures neither the scheduler nor the transfer.
- [ ] Measure the wake path: a channel becomes readable, and the thread waiting on it runs. This is
  the term every "the service was not scheduled" explanation rests on, and the kernel wake path note
  in `docs/PERF.md` is from 2026-07-06 and predates every service on this path.
- [ ] Measure what a page costs: mapped, unmapped, and copied. A frame at 640x480 is 300 pages, so a
  per-page cost of even a microsecond is a millisecond a frame, and whether the present path pays it
  once, twice or not at all is the difference between the account closing and not.
- [ ] Measure one syscall's floor, so that a layer's cost can be stated in syscalls where that is what
  it is made of.
- [ ] EVERY ONE OF THESE IS MEASURED IN THE GUEST, under the scheduler and the address spaces the
  real path runs under. A host-side number for any of them belongs in a different document.

## P02M0189 - The comparison that makes a number mean something

- [ ] Run the same drawing work three ways and put the three side by side: in one process with no
  service and no transport, which is the floor the benchmark suites already measure; through the real
  path with one application; and through the real path with the display doing what a desktop makes it
  do. The difference between the first two is what this system charges for being a system, and it is
  the single number this milestone is for.
- [ ] STATE THE CHARGE AS A RATIO AND AS A COST PER FRAME, both, because they answer different
  questions. A path that adds 50 ms to a 5 ms frame and one that adds 50 ms to a 500 ms frame are the
  same absolute finding and opposite priorities.
- [ ] Compare against nothing outside this tree. There is no second operating system on this machine
  to be measured against, and a number quoted from a specification or from memory is not a
  measurement. What this milestone compares is this system against itself with layers removed.

## P02M0189 - The verdict

- [ ] Name the dominant term, in one sentence, with its share of the frame. If the largest term is
  the renderer after all, that is the finding and it is written down as plainly as any other - the
  hypothesis that opened this milestone is a hypothesis.
- [ ] For every term above five percent of the frame, say which of four things it is: work that has to
  happen, work that happens more often than it has to, waiting that is a scheduling consequence, or
  work this system does the long way because it has no primitive for the short way. THE FOURTH IS THE
  ONE THIS MILESTONE WAS ASKED FOR, and each instance of it becomes a named follow-up item stating
  what the missing primitive is and what it would remove. It does not implement one.
- [ ] Write the account into `docs/PERF.md` as one section with the date, the instrument's cost, the
  table of terms, the residue, and the four-way classification. A finding that lives only in a
  milestone is a finding the next person measuring this path will not read.
- [ ] Record every disproof. A term that was expected to be large and was not is worth exactly as
  much as one that was, and this tree already keeps disproofs for the renderer - the transfer-encode
  memo, the filter-node span rewrite - for the reason that the next attempt starts knowing.

## P02M0189 - The one exception to "measure, do not fix"

- [ ] A change is in scope here ONLY where the measurement is impossible without it: a term that
  cannot be isolated because two layers share one timestamp, a boundary with no observable crossing,
  a clock too coarse to resolve it. Such a change is minimal, is named in the account as a change
  made for measurement, and carries the number that justified it.
- [ ] Anything else found on the way is written down and left alone. A milestone that finds a
  ten-line fix and takes it has spent its remaining measurements defending that fix.

## P02M0189 - Gates and completion

- [ ] The instrument's host-testable parts close on host tests: the frame identity, the buffer's
  bounds, the join, and the refusal to record past the bound. A ring that silently drops the last
  frame of every run is an instrument that reports the frames nobody cared about.
- [ ] One in-guest gate produces the account on x86_64 and fails if the residue exceeds the five
  percent above, or if any term is missing its work/wait pair. The gate is the thing that keeps the
  account true after the next change to any layer in it.
- [ ] The emulated ports run LAST, once x86_64's account closes, and only to check it. Their numbers
  go in the same section, marked as what they are.
- [ ] No milestone item here is closed by an argument. Every one of them closes on a number, and a
  number that was not measured in this tree by a command in this tree is not one.
