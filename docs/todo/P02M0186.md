# P02M0186 - A spool and stream service

Status: OPEN. It closes when every item below is done, and not before.

**APPLICATIONS RECEIVE A JOB CAPABILITY, NOT RAW ENDPOINTS.** That is P02M0099's USB printer item
in its own words, and it is the whole reason this is a service. A printer reached as a bulk endpoint
is a device any holder can drive into any state; a printer reached as a JOB is a thing with a
beginning, an end, a status and an owner - and a second application cannot interleave its bytes
into the middle of somebody else's page.

**AND IT IS DELIBERATELY NARROW AT FIRST.** The driver item is explicit about what stays out: page
description and rendering, discovery UI, and vendor maintenance protocols live above or outside.
What this service owns is the spool - a queue of jobs, their bytes, their status and their
lifetime.

**AND IT EXISTS BECAUSE THE OWNER SAID SO (2026-09-21)**, answering P02M0099's question about the
nine unowned destinations.

## The shape

- [ ] A typed `liber:spool@1` interface: devices, their status, and a job. Creating a job returns a
  capability; writing to it appends; closing it commits. Nothing hands out the device.
- [ ] BACKPRESSURE IS THE INTERFACE AND NOT A DETAIL. A printer consumes bytes at the speed of
  paper, and a writer that is never told to wait is a writer whose buffer grows until something
  breaks. The write answers, in the same shape the console stream contract already uses: `again` is
  a device that has not taken these bytes and will take them later.
- [ ] Status that says what happened rather than that something did: out of paper, cover open,
  jammed, offline - and unplugged mid-job as its own outcome, because a job that ended because the
  printer left is not a job that failed to print.
- [ ] Job lifetime and quota: how many jobs may be queued, how large one may be, and what happens
  to a job whose owner exits. Each declared rather than discovered.
- [ ] Already-rendered languages first, which is what the driver item asks for, and a typed refusal
  for anything else rather than a best effort.
- [ ] Host tests over the status decode and the job state machine, including every way a job can
  end.
- [ ] A guest gate over the observable effect, registered in the verification model in the same
  change.

## Definition of done

Done when an application holding a job capability can write a rendered document with honest
backpressure and read its status through to an outcome; when no client path carries a raw endpoint;
when job quotas and owner-exit behaviour are declared and tested; and when the host tests and the
guest gate land with the code.
