# P02M0027 - LiberFS: transparent compression (optional, last)

Status: COMPLETE. **Deliberately the last filesystem feature**, after extents, B+trees and snapshots
were solid.

*Condensed 2026-08-08.*

Compression is per extent, which is why it waited for the extent map: compress on write, store the
algorithm and the compressed length beside the extent, decompress on read, and **store an
incompressible extent raw** rather than paying to make it slightly larger.

The codec is vendored and dependency-free, per the `no_std` zero-dependency rule.

**Checksums cover the stored - compressed - bytes**, which is the right choice: it verifies what the
medium actually holds, so damage is caught before decompression rather than as a confusing failure
inside it.

Two features are out **by decision, not by omission**: deduplication is too costly for what it would
buy here, and encryption belongs to a lower block or volume layer.
