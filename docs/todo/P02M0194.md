# P02M0194 - Bluetooth, complete: classic and LE, every desktop profile, audio and voice

Status: OPEN. It closes when every part below is done. Started 2026-10-05 on the owner's go-ahead for every
open milestone: part a's foundations are in and gated on x86_64 (`bluetooth-classic`); the rest follows in the
milestone's order.

THE ORACLE, AS IT STANDS (2026-10-05). Adopting RootCanal and Bumble is the owner's question for when part a
starts, and it is recorded for him rather than assumed: until he answers, every gate here runs on the IN-GUEST
FIXTURE, WRITTEN BY THIS TEAM AND SAID SO. Its BR/EDR half (`drivers::bt_world`) is a dual-mode controller's
classic side and five devices - a phone, a keyboard, a headset, a serial device and a legacy PIN device - whose
L2CAP, SDP, RFCOMM (its CRC included, held to the canonical SABM and UA frames) and pairing half are written
apart from the host stack's `service_logic`; it is driven through a `fixture-control` publication only a
development probe's policy row grants.

THE OWNER'S DECISION (2026-09-25), IN HIS WORDS: a new milestone that finishes the service COMPLETELY, for the
whole of Bluetooth. It follows his "yes" to Bluetooth voice: the SCO transport is planned in P02M0099, and he
was told that voice is useless without a host stack that can set a headset's call up - which P02M0180, scoped
to one LE mouse, does not have. So this milestone takes the service from "one encrypted LE boot mouse" to what
a desktop operating system's Bluetooth does.

WHAT EXISTS, and this milestone builds on it rather than beside it:
  - THE TRANSPORT (P02M0099): HCI over USB - commands, events and ACL with their bounds, stall and unplug
    recovery, epochs across a reset - and, planned there, SCO over the isochronous alternates. ISO is a
    reserved kind nothing carries, and no P02M0099 item owns carrying it: part h does, both halves.
  - THE SERVICE (P02M0180): a sandboxed process with no device authority; LE scanning, one LE central
    connection, LE Secure Connections pairing with Just Works, a durable bond store, ATT/GATT discovery for
    the HID-over-GATT boot mouse, the read / operator / profile scopes, bounded tables, and `btctl`.
  - THE FIXTURES: an in-guest HCI controller-and-peer provider on the production wire, a FunctionFS
    controller in the harness, and `usbredir_device.py`, which has the isochronous endpoints a gadget lacks.
  - THE CONSUMERS, each built for less than this milestone hands them: InputService opens one mouse stream from
    the first enabled peer; AudioService drives one device at a fixed 48 kHz stereo with one recorder and no
    inventory, volume, latency or voice; NetworkService runs one uplink, an Ethernet publication or a raw-IP
    link ModemService installs; and no application-facing serial port exists.

WHAT "COMPLETE" MEANS HERE, stated so it can be checked: every profile a desktop user meets - input devices,
music, calls, serial links, file push, tethering and LE Audio - on both radios, with the pairing models the
specification defines (Out Of Band excepted: this machine has no out-of-band channel to carry its data) and the
security it requires. EXCLUDED as not a desktop's: Bluetooth Mesh, the peripheral (server) role beyond what the
profiles need, proprietary codecs (aptX, LDAC) and AAC, whose use is licensed.

HOW WHAT THE STACK PRODUCES REACHES ITS OWNERS (decided 2026-09-26). ONLY A DRIVER BINDING PUBLISHES INTO THE
CATALOGUE, and BluetoothService is no driver: it publishes nothing, and no path for publishers without a device
is built - P02M0180's rule against a pretend provider stands. Every delivery is a NAMED CONTRACT on a root of
BluetoothService's own, which its one consumer reaches BY NAME THROUGH THE BROKER, exactly as InputService
reaches `bluetooth-profile` today, and not through a role: a role is a dependency, a stop takes the reverse
closure, and stopping the radio's stack must stop no audio, network or input service - nor may any of them wait
for a radio to start. Each is a new root in the manifest, a broker name in `capability_names.rs` and one row in
ServiceManager's grant table, additive in `liber:bluetooth@1` at version 1:

    contract            consumer            carries
    bluetooth-profile   InputService        decoded pointer, key, consumer-control and gamepad reports from
                                            every peer trusted for input (part c; grown from `open-mouse`)
    bluetooth-audio     AudioService        audio endpoints as they come and go, each opened as a PCM channel
                                            with its format and latency; volume both ways; the voice link and
                                            the call relay (part d)
    bluetooth-network   NetworkService      PAN links as they come and go, each opened as a frame channel
                                            speaking a NIC provider's frame-plus-link wire (part g)
    bluetooth-admin     PermissionManager   the minting root for per-launch application grants: an SPP byte
                                            stream (`bluetooth-serial`, part g) and a GATT client
                                            (`bluetooth-gatt`, part b), each for one component and one peer

LOSS AND RESTART, ONE RULE FOR ALL FOUR. A consumer's open channels end with the BluetoothService instance that
served them and the consumer re-resolves, as InputService's slot already does; the new instance offers again
what its devices bring back and revives nothing. A restarted consumer re-resolves and reopens what is still
offered. When a channel ends, InputService releases every held key and button, AudioService moves the device's
streams by its routing rule, NetworkService withdraws the link as it withdraws a NIC (a new interface
generation), and a granted application sees its stream end.

ORDER: part i's oracle is built first; then a, b and c (c's gamepad half after P02M0192's vocabulary); d before
e, f and h; P02M0099's SCO transport item before part f's gate; part h's own ISO transport item before part h's
gate.

## P02M0194a - Classic (BR/EDR) foundations, bounds and pairing

- [x] THE BOUNDS, STATED BEFORE ANY CODE, replacing P02M0180's one-link table. The Domain is derived from them;
  part i's re-measurement checks the derivation and is not its source.

    links        two controllers, as before; at most 8 ACL links per controller across both radios, at most 7
                 of them BR/EDR (a piconet's active members); the controller's own smaller count wins
    per link     16 L2CAP channels, at most 2 of them in enhanced retransmission mode; 8 RFCOMM channels in its
                 one RFCOMM session; 32 queued ACL packets
    SDU / MTU    1691 bytes on every BR/EDR dynamic channel - BNEP's minimum MTU, and above what AVDTP, AVCTP,
                 HID, RFCOMM and OBEX need; OBEX's maximum packet is offered as the same 1691. ATT MTU 247 on
                 LE - above BAP's minimum of 64, and one Data Length Extension PDU with its L2CAP header. One
                 incomplete SDU per channel and the one-second assembly deadline, as before
    ERTM         for AVRCP browsing and OBEX over L2CAP: a transmit window of 8 and the specification's default
                 2-second retransmission and 12-second monitor time-outs; streaming mode is not used
    synchronous  one SCO/eSCO link per controller; 4 connected isochronous streams and one broadcast group of
                 at most 2 streams; ISO SDUs within the controller's buffer and the 4100-byte transport ceiling
    storage      1.5 MB aggregate packet storage, SHARED BY BOTH CONTROLLERS as P02M0180's aggregate is, against
                 the worst cases of their 16 links together - 16 x 16 x 1691 = 433 kB of reassembly,
                 16 x 2 x 8 x 1691 = 433 kB of ERTM windows and 16 x 32 x 1028 = 526 kB of queued ACL, about
                 1.39 MB; 16 client connections and 32 minted grants; bonds 64 records in 64 kB, unchanged - a
                 record grows by keys and trust bits to about 200 bytes
    Domain       memory 64 MB, 256 handles, 4 threads and 2 MB stacks stay, every table above being well inside
                 them; IPC queues 2 MB -> 4 MB for the new standing queues - a PAN link's 64 frames each way
                 and four audio endpoints' 16 periods each way, about 0.7 MB

  the operator authority: a typed record (controller, peer, model, the six-digit value or the passkey to show,
  keypress notifications as they arrive, a deadline) answered with yes or no, a passkey or a PIN. `btctl` holds
  it, as it holds every operator verb, and TAKES THE SHELL'S INTERACTIVE LAUNCH SHAPE, the one `play` and

  answers there, which the output-only shape it launches with today cannot. THE DEADLINE IS THE PROTOCOL'S: 25
  seconds, five inside the 30-second Security Manager and LMP response timers, after which the service answers
  no itself. WHAT THIS HOST DECLARES while a prompt watcher is attached (a running `btctl pair` or `btctl
  pairable`, one per controller): KeyboardDisplay on LE, and DisplayYesNo on BR/EDR, whose IO capabilities
  have no KeyboardDisplay - so on BR/EDR this host shows the passkey a keyboard types and never types one
  itself, and Passkey Entry in both directions is LE's. With no watcher it declares NoInputNoOutput on both, so
  no model is offered that nobody can answer. NOT P02M0188'S PROTECTED SCREEN: it confirms an operation a
  requester asked for and an executor froze, not a value a protocol produces midway that nobody requested; it
  waits 60 seconds against a 30-second timer; it has no entry; and it exists only with a USB or virtio
  keyboard, so a machine whose only keyboard is Bluetooth could never pair one. What an answer proves is stated
  rather than implied: the holder of the operator authority confirmed, on an ordinary terminal another local
  program could overdraw - a limit `docs/THREAT_MODEL.md` states, beside the fact that Numeric Comparison and

    the in-guest fixture, written by the same team and stated as such.
  - WHEN PART e STARTS, once for all three codecs: shipping SBC and mSBC, and LC3 in part h, on a product that
    is not SIG-qualified. The SIG's royalty-free patent licence covers the compliant portions of a QUALIFIED
    product, which is the question already answered for AAC by excluding it. And whether the SIG's member-only
    conformance material is obtained under its terms.
