# P02M0184 - A camera vocabulary and service

Status: OPEN. It closes when every item below is done, and not before.

**AND IT IS NOT DISPLAYSERVICE, WHICH THE DRIVER ITEM REFUSES IN AS MANY WORDS.** P02M0099's UVC
item says it explicitly: do not tunnel video through DisplayService. A display is a sink this system
owns and drives; a camera is a source somebody else's hardware fills, at a rate it chooses, with
frames a client may or may not be allowed to see. Sending the second through the first would make
every camera policy question a display question.

**SO THE VOCABULARY COMES FIRST, AND THAT IS THE ORDER THE DRIVER ITEM ASKS FOR.** "First add a
bounded camera vocabulary for format/frame enumeration, negotiated stream parameters, timestamps and
capability-scoped frame buffers." A driver written before that vocabulary exists would invent one,
and the one it invented would be the shape UVC happens to have.

**AND IT EXISTS BECAUSE THE OWNER SAID SO (2026-09-21)**, answering P02M0099's question about the
nine unowned destinations.

## The shape

- [ ] A typed `liber:camera@1` interface: devices, a bounded enumeration of formats and frame
  sizes and rates, a negotiated stream, and frames delivered as capability-scoped buffers with
  timestamps. Buffers rather than copies, because a frame path that copies every frame is a frame
  path that decides the system's throughput.
- [ ] TIMESTAMPS ARE THE DEVICE'S AND THE ARRIVAL IS OURS, and both are carried. A consumer that
  cannot tell "this frame was captured 40 ms ago" from "this frame arrived 40 ms late" cannot do
  anything with either.
- [ ] Frame loss reported rather than hidden. A dropped frame is a gap in a sequence number, and a
  stream that renumbered to look continuous is one nobody can reason about.
- [ ] OPENING A CAMERA IS A CAPABILITY AND AN ACT. A camera that any process holding the service
  connection may start is a camera that starts without anybody deciding to; the permission portal
  `docs/CONCEPT_EN.md` lists under LATER is the consumer of this, not a substitute for it.
- [ ] No image decoder here and none in the driver: consumers use the existing codec leaves. What
  this service carries is the frame and its metadata.
- [ ] Host tests over the format and frame enumeration and the negotiation decisions, with the
  malformed descriptor cases.
- [ ] A guest gate over an emulated camera, registered in the verification model in the same change.

## Definition of done

Done when a client holding a camera capability can enumerate formats, negotiate a stream and
receive bounded frames with both timestamps and honest loss reporting; when starting a camera
requires an explicit act and not merely a connection; when the enumeration and negotiation are
host-tested against malformed descriptors; and when the guest gate and its model entry land with
the code.

## What it unblocks

P02M0099's **USB Video Class (UVC)**.
