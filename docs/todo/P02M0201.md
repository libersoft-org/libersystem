# P02M0201 - Out-of-band management over IPMI: KCS, BT and SSIF, and a BMC service with a tool

Status: OPEN. Created 2026-09-25 under the owner's approval of P02M0196 - "yes, do all of it; where a
milestone is missing, create it". NOTHING HERE IS STARTED: the owner asked for plans only until he says
otherwise, and no part starts without its own written plan.

WHY. A server's baseboard management controller (BMC) is a small computer beside the main one: it reads the
board's sensors, keeps an event log, powers the machine on, off and through a cycle, and answers the network
when the operating system cannot. The operating system talks to it IN-BAND, over IPMI's system interfaces.
P02M0099 lists "both IPMI transports" with NO destination - "IPMI is the out-of-band path that works when the
system does not"; this milestone is that destination and the transports under it.

WHAT QEMU OFFERS, checked on this machine (10.0.11) and against QEMU 10.0's sources: a simulated BMC
(`ipmi-bmc-sim`, which takes SDR and FRU data from files the harness writes, and answers Get Device GUID only
when given `guid=` - the property has no default, and an unset GUID is answered 0xC1) or an external one
(`ipmi-bmc-extern`, a chardev to a BMC program), and every system interface - `isa-ipmi-kcs` (0xCA2, IRQ 5),
`isa-ipmi-bt` (0xE4, IRQ 5), `pci-ipmi-kcs` and `pci-ipmi-bt` (class 0x0C07, interface 0x01 and 0x02, their
registers in an I/O-SPACE BAR 0, INTx only), and `smbus-ipmi` for SSIF on the SMBus of q35's ICH9 function
(`8086:2930` at 00:1f.3, class 0x0C05, whose SMBus base is the I/O-space BAR 4, decoded only while
HOSTC.HST_EN - bit 0 of configuration offset 0x40 - is set). QEMU describes the ISA and SMBus interfaces the
way real firmware does: an `IPI0001` device in its DSDT (`_IFT`, `_SRV`, and a `_CRS` holding the I/O range,
or an `I2cSerialBusV2` whose resource source is the SMBus function's own node) and an SMBIOS type 38 record,
whose base is the I/O address with bit 0 set for I/O space, or the SSIF address shifted left by one. The PCI
forms need no description and get no type 38 record.

WHAT IT CANNOT SHOW, and therefore what the gates do instead (P02M0201c):
  - CHASSIS CONTROL: QEMU performs hard reset (a system reset), power down (a shutdown request) and soft
    shutdown (an ACPI power-button press), and answers power cycle and power up with 0xD5, "not supported".
  - THE SIMULATOR'S WATCHDOG calls QEMU's chassis reset directly on expiry. It never reaches QEMU's watchdog
    layer, so there is no QMP `WATCHDOG` event and `-action watchdog=` does not apply. The simulator has no
    reset handler, so its state - the expiration flags included - survives the reset.
  - THE INTERFACE MODELS REWRITE A HOSTILE BMC before the guest sees it: KCS and BT turn an answer past their
    300-byte buffers into 0xCA, SSIF truncates at 255 bytes with 0xC6, BT writes back the sequence number the
    guest sent and drops an answer nobody awaits, and `ipmi-bmc-extern` turns a silent BMC into 0xC3 after four
    seconds and a disconnected one into 0xD2.
  - The simulator implements no LAN-configuration, user or chassis-identify command, and gives the harness no
    way to change a sensor reading.

WHO HOLDS WHAT. Only a driver holding a claim publishes into the catalogue, so the process graph is:
  - `ipmi`, ONE DRIVER PROGRAM, one process per bound system interface. It holds the claim (a port range, a
    PCI BAR, or one address on an SMBus), the message layer, and every publication the BMC feeds: `ipmi` - a
    new provider kind whose one consumer is the BMC service - `power-source` (P02M0181), `watchdog`
    (P02M0200; never from the SSIF form) and two `admin-executor`s (P02M0188).
  - `smbus_ich9`, the ICH9 SMBus host driver, publishing P02M0195's `i2c-bus` provider that SSIF uses.
  - `bmc_service`, THE BMC SERVICE: it holds no claim and publishes nothing. It consumes `ipmi` through a
    catalogue connection minted for that kind alone, writes the system's own events, and serves the tool.
  - `bmc`, the shipping tool.
  PowerService, P02M0200's watchdog service and AdminService reach this driver's publications exactly as
  they reach every other publication of those kinds.

WHAT IT WAITS FOR, AND IN WHAT ORDER:
  - the PCI forms: P02M0191's `PortRange` and its PCI I/O BAR source (the BAR recorded by the kernel's own
    scan, minted with the PCI claim, I/O decode enabled at claim). They need no firmware description, so they
    come FIRST;
  - the ISA forms: also P02M0196a's platform device with its port resources (the `_CRS` range checked by the
    kernel before it is minted) and P02M0196b's namespace - QEMU's `IPI0001` is AML in the DSDT - with the
    node-scoped ACPI channel the claim carries;
  - SSIF: also P02M0195's bus half (the contract with its functionality set, and the child binding) and
    P02M0196's companion join, which attaches `\_SB.PCI0.SFB` to the ICH9 function and resolves the
    `I2cSerialBusV2` resource source to that function's binding and `i2c-bus` provider;
  - P02M0201b: P02M0200a's `watchdog` kind, its orderly-shutdown notice and its way of delivering a channel
    ServiceManager serves as a plan-resolved role, P02M0188's administrative path, and P02M0181's
    `power-source` kind and `power-model` leaf (both landed);
  - the SMBIOS cross-check: P02M0196a's SMBIOS item. Nothing waits for it.

## P02M0201a - The transports

- [ ] THE KERNEL'S RESOLVER ROWS, this milestone's own, on top of P02M0191's PCI I/O BAR source (which
  records every function's I/O BARs on its row and mints them with the ordinary PCI claim, I/O decode set at
  claim and cleared at release):
    - class 0x0C/0x07 with interface 0x01 (KCS) and 0x02 (BT), naming BAR 0 as the register file: an I/O BAR
      reaches the driver as that source's `PortRange`, a memory BAR as the existing MMIO grant. Interface
      0x00 (SMIC) gets no row;
    - `8086:2930`, the ICH9 SMBus function, naming BAR 4 as the SMBus base - keyed by vendor and device, not
      by class, because other SMBus hosts share the class and not the registers. It lives in the kernel's
      (vendor, device)-keyed resolver table, which whichever of P02M0200 and this milestone lands first adds;
      it uses none of P02M0200's declared registers. THE KERNEL WRITES HOSTC AT CLAIM: it saves the
      register, sets HST_EN and clears I2C_EN, and RESTORES the saved value at release - the Linux
      `i2c-i801` sequence - because the BAR decodes nothing while HST_EN is clear. An S3 sleep may lose it
      with the rest of the function's configuration, so the kernel writes the claim's value again after an
      S3, beside P02M0197b's restore of the function's header - P02M0197b's restore list holds every
      configuration register the kernel writes for a claim - CARRIED BY WHICHEVER OF P02M0197 AND THIS
      MILESTONE LANDS SECOND, the first to land recording that it is owed. No gate shows it on QEMU, whose
      ICH9 SMBus model sets HOSTC only when the device is realized and keeps it through the wakeup's device
      reset, which clears the command register, the status and the BARs but not offset 0x40; hardware does
      lose it, which is why Linux sets HST_EN again at resume. No runtime configuration write exists and
      none is needed.
- [ ] KCS AND BT STATE MACHINES in a host-tested library, `src/user/libs/driver/ipmi`, over a register trait
  that port and memory access both implement: KCS's write and read phases on IBF and OBF, its error state and
  the ABORT sequence; BT's host-to-BMC and BMC-to-host handshakes, `Get BT Interface Capabilities` first, and a
  response whose sequence number, network function or command is not the request's refused. EVERY FORM
  POLLS: a status wait spins a bounded number of reads, then sleeps one tick between reads. The interrupt a
  claim may carry stays masked. Register spacing is 1 on the PCI forms (as QEMU and Linux use) and on the ISA
  forms follows from the claimed port range (its length over the interface's register count).
- [ ] `smbus_ich9`, THE ICH9 SMBUS HOST DRIVER, serving P02M0195's bus contract and declaring the part of its
  functionality set SSIF uses: SMBus BLOCK WRITE, BLOCK READ returning the count the DEVICE sends, and PEC
  (ICH9's automatic PEC). It declares NO plain I2C transfer - the controller performs SMBus protocols only -
  so a HID-over-I2C device is refused on it at bind. Its other SMBus protocols (quick, byte, word, I2C block
  read) are declared when a consumer needs one. It polls the host status register and uses no interrupt and
  no SMBus alert. It binds on every q35 boot, since QEMU always has the function; idle, it touches no register.
- [ ] SSIF OVER THAT CONTRACT, as P02M0195's CHILD BINDING: the `IPI0001` node below the SMBus function's
  node is a method-only platform device the `ipmi` driver claims, and its connection is a requirement naming
  the ICH9 function, so it binds after its parent. DeviceManager mints, through the controller's `CONNECT`,
  the address-scoped connection for the `I2cSerialBusV2` address and hands it over with the claim - the
  driver holds one address, never the bus, and the controller enforces it - and stops the child as a lost
  dependency when the SMBus binding ends. SSIF runs on ICH9 and NOT on virtio-i2c, which cannot serve a block
  read whose length the device sends. The protocol: `Get System Interface Capabilities` first (the largest
  request and response, multi-part support, PEC support); single-part and multi-part writes (start, middle,
  end); single-part and multi-part reads (the first block, middle blocks with their block numbers, the end);
  bounded retries on a NACK while the BMC prepares its answer; PEC only when both the BMC and the controller
  offer it. It POLLS for the answer: there is no alert line.
- [ ] ONE MESSAGE LAYER OVER THE THREE:
    - network function, command and completion code; sequence numbers where the transport has them;
    - A RESPONSE IS BOUNDED at 272 bytes - network function, command, completion code and data - Linux's
      `IPMI_MAX_MSG_LENGTH`; on SSIF it is also no more than the BMC's reported response size;
    - A TRANSACTION HAS A DEADLINE of five seconds, above `ipmi-bmc-extern`'s four so that QEMU's own timeout
      answer arrives first; a KCS interface still busy at the deadline is aborted within one second;
    - THE BMC UNAVAILABLE: three transactions in a row ending in 0xC3, 0xD2 or the deadline mark the BMC
      unavailable - the `ipmi` provider says so, the thermal zones become unknown - rather than a hang; Get
      Device ID every ten seconds brings it back, and a different IDENTITY on return (P02M0201b: the GUID, or
      for a BMC that answers none its manufacturer, product and device ID) is a different BMC, whose
      repositories are read again;
    - ONE MESSAGE AT A TIME, AND THE PET FIRST. KCS, BT (QEMU's reports one outstanding request) and SSIF
      carry one request at a time, so the driver keeps ONE queue in front of the interface. A watchdog pet or
      re-arm goes to its head and never waits behind a queued read, and every long read is cut into bounded
      transactions: an SDR record in partial reads, FRU data in 32-byte reads, one SEL entry per request. The
      longest a pet waits is one transaction and one abort, six seconds; the watchdog's shortest timeout
      (15 s, P02M0201b) is three deadlines, so a stuck transaction, its recovery and the pet fit inside one
      timeout.
- [ ] DISCOVERY. The PCI forms by class 0x0C07 with interface 0x01 or 0x02. The ISA and SSIF forms by the
  `IPI0001` namespace device ALONE, which is sufficient: the driver matches `_HID` `IPI0001` and reads `_IFT`
  (1 KCS, 3 BT, 4 SSIF; 2, SMIC, refused) and `_SRV` through the node-scoped ACPI channel, its resources
  coming with the claim. SMBIOS TYPE 38 IS A CROSS-CHECK, NEVER A ROW, through P02M0196a, which carries SMBIOS
  to the system: a record is attached to the `IPI0001` row whose interface and base agree, as that row's
  `smbios:38#n` match id - for KCS and BT when the record's base, read as the specification lays it out (bit
  0 the I/O-space flag, the address's own bit 0 in the base address modifier), equals the `_CRS` range's
  base; for SSIF, whose record names no controller, when the base shifted right by one - the 7-bit address,
  which the record keeps left-justified - equals the `I2cSerialBusV2` address. A record that matches no node,
  or disagrees, is reported and changes nothing: it never merges into the node's resources and never refuses
  the node, which is used either way. A machine without SMBIOS (every direct device-tree boot) loses nothing.
- [ ] THE MANIFEST DECLARATIONS. `ipmi`: matches the two PCI class rows (`transport = "plain-pci"`, class
  0x0C, subclass 0x07, interface 0x01 or 0x02) and `_HID` `IPI0001` in P02M0196a's match vocabulary;
  `dma = "none"`; provides `ipmi` (most 1, consumers 1), `power-source` (most 1, consumers 1), `watchdog`
  (most 1, consumers 1; offered by the KCS, BT and PCI forms, never by SSIF - below) and `admin-executor`
  (most 2, consumers 1). `ipmi` is appended to `provider-kind` at the next unused value, version 1, and the
  catalogue admits it to the BMC service's kind-scoped connection alone. `smbus_ich9`: matches
  `transport = "plain-pci", pci-class = 0x0C, pci-subclass = 0x05, pci-interface = 0x00`, narrowed by
  `pci-vendor = 0x8086, pci-product = 0x2930` - the manifest refuses a rule of vendor and product alone,
  which may only narrow a standard identity, and a `plain-pci` rule without a class; `dma = "none"`;
  provides `i2c-bus` (most 1, consumers 8), whose offered endpoint serves nothing and is closed at
  publication without being counted, as for every `i2c-bus` row: its only connections are the
  address-scoped ones DeviceManager mints.
  TWO INTERFACES TO ONE BMC - two bindings answering ONE Device GUID; a BMC that answers none is never part of
  a pair, its identity including its binding (P02M0201b) - both bind and both publish, and the BMC service
  reports the pair, so an operator can disable one with DeviceManager's `disable` verb. Nothing
  de-duplicates automatically. AdminService adopts the first executor for each action and refuses the
  second, its existing rule, and both name the same target. A pair of register interfaces (SSIF publishes
  none) offers two `bmc` providers for ONE timer, and P02M0200's watchdog service opens ONE provider per
  device name: the second `bmc` provider is left unopened and reported, never disarmed, so nothing done
  through one interface stops what was armed through the other; the BMC service's report of the pair names
  the interface an operator may disable.
- [ ] SUSPEND AND RESUME. Both drivers implement P02M0197's exchange and declare its `suspend-deadline`,
  carried by whichever of P02M0197 and this milestone lands SECOND - P02M0197 refuses a sleep while a binding
  without one is Online, and `smbus_ich9` binds on every q35 boot. `ipmi` on `SUSPEND` finishes the
  transaction in flight within its deadline (a KCS one past it aborted) and holds its queue; on `RESUME` it
  aborts a KCS interface left busy, reads BT's capabilities and the BMC's identity again - a BMC can reset
  while the host sleeps - and then releases the queue; its `watchdog` step is P02M0200's (the BMC's timer can
  be disarmed, so it is, and one that was armed is armed again when the binding resumes first). `smbus_ich9`
  finishes the transfer in flight, holds new ones and touches no register until `RESUME`; after S3 on hardware
  the kernel's HOSTC write (above) makes its BAR decode again. THE SSIF FORM PUBLISHES NO `watchdog`: P02M0197
  suspends a watchdog's binding LAST and resumes it FIRST, and a child of the SMBus controller - suspended
  before its controller and resumed after it - can be neither; the KCS, BT and PCI forms publish it.

## P02M0201b - The BMC service, its tool and its administrative actions

- [ ] `liber:bmc@1`, additive and version 1: the `ipmi-provider` contract (driver to BMC service) and the
  `bmc` client interface (BMC service to tool), over shared records, every list bounded. NEITHER CARRIES A RAW
  COMMAND, AND NEITHER CAN STOP THE MACHINE OR ERASE THE LOG: those are the executor publications', which only
  AdminService reaches. The provider contract's only writes are `system-event`, limited to the two events
  below and built by the driver, and the identify light.
  THE BMC SERVICE'S MANIFEST ROW, as PowerService's: `bmc_service`, restart transparent and state
  reconstructible (a restarted instance re-reads every `ipmi` provider), and named among ServiceManager's
  plan-relaunchable services, since every role it has is plan-resolved; depending on LogService,
  ProcessService and DeviceManager; roles - a `provider-catalogue` factory from DeviceManager with
  `kinds = ["ipmi"]`, the supervisor read channel below, and its own serve root `liber:bmc@1/bmc`; and the
  declaration that it takes P02M0200's orderly-shutdown notice. ITS CATALOGUE ROLE TAKES TWO SLOTS of the
  manifest's 32-slot catalogue-client bound, as every transparent service's minting role does - a restart
  mints the replacement before the old channel closes - so today's demand of 27 becomes 29. Whichever of
  P02M0190, P02M0198, P02M0199, P02M0200, P02M0201 and P02M0202 first pushes the demand past 32 raises the
  bound to 64 in the system-manifest crate and in DeviceManager's table in the same change.
- [ ] THE SUPERVISOR READ CHANNEL, delivered by the plan: ServiceManager serves the existing `supervisor`
  interface of `liber:observability@1` (`status` - each managed service's state, wanted state and restart
  count) from its standing loop's wait set, on a channel the plan delivers to the BMC service as a role and
  mints again for a replacement, as P02M0200a delivers its `supervisor-liveness` channel. The `SUPERVISOR`
  channels that exist are built by hand for SystemGraphService and PermissionManager alone, and a relaunched
  service gets back only what the plan delivers.
- [ ] Identity: Get Device ID (device ID and revision, firmware revision, IPMI version, manufacturer,
  product) and Get Device GUID, which IPMI makes OPTIONAL and QEMU's simulated BMC answers only when given
  `guid=`. THE BMC'S IDENTITY is its GUID; a BMC that answers Get Device GUID with an error, or with sixteen
  zero bytes, is named by its manufacturer, product and device ID TOGETHER WITH ITS BINDING - the PCI address
  or the `acpi:` path of the device its interface is bound to - so two BMCs without a GUID are never taken for
  one. The identity names the BMC in the tool and in the administrative target - `bmc:` and the GUID's 32 hex
  digits, or `bmc:` and the fallback, within the selector's 64 bytes (a longer fallback leaves that BMC
  unadministrable, and the tool says so) - and only BMCs that answer a GUID can form a pair (P02M0201a).
- [ ] SENSORS FROM THE SDR REPOSITORY - full and compact records; event-only records listed without a
  reading; locator records naming the FRU devices; at most 512 records and 256 sensors, a larger repository
  reported as truncated and never read past - each with its reading, unit, thresholds and state, for the
  `bmc` tool: temperatures, fans, voltages, power supplies and the rest.
  TEMPERATURES ALSO REACH P02M0181 AS `thermal-zone` SOURCES - at most sixteen, the first in repository
  order - through a new adapter in the `power-model` leaf, `power_model::ipmi`: the SDR's linear conversion (M,
  B, the B and R exponents; unsigned, one's- or two's-complement readings) in checked integer arithmetic;
  degrees C, F or K to millidegrees Celsius; a non-linear formula, or a sensor with no analog reading,
  `unsupported`; "reading unavailable" and "scanning disabled" `unknown`; the upper non-critical, critical
  and non-recoverable thresholds as `passive`, `hot` and `critical` trip points; `over-temperature` as a
  REPORTED alarm at or above upper critical. The library calls the same conversion for every other threshold
  sensor, so there is one. The sixteen are polled every five seconds and an update is published when one
  changes. Trip points are observations: P02M0198's thermal policy acts on the `thermal-zone` provider kind an
  ACPI zone's own driver publishes, not on these sources, and the BMC enforces its own thresholds.
  POWER SUPPLIES STAY IN THE TOOL: they are discrete sensors, and P02M0181's source kinds - AC, battery, UPS,
  load, thermal zone - include no server power supply.
- [ ] The System Event Log: its entry count from Get SEL Info, read afresh when the tool asks; its entries in
  pages of sixteen (system event records decoded - timestamp, generator, sensor type and number, event type
  and data; OEM records shown as bytes); and FOLLOWED by polling Get SEL Info's most recent addition every
  five seconds. Clearing it is an administrative action below, and a request to clear it carries that count.
- [ ] FRU inventory, per FRU device the locator records name (at most eight): the common header's and each
  area's checksum, the board, chassis and product areas' type/length fields (binary, BCD plus, six-bit ASCII,
  eight-bit), each length checked against its area before a byte is believed; the multi-record area listed as
  present, not decoded.
- [ ] THE SYSTEM'S OWN EVENTS INTO THAT LOG - the log of every BMC the service holds, once per BMC and a
  pair's through one of its interfaces - each a Platform Event Message the DRIVER builds and sends (generator
  0x41, system management software) when the BMC service asks:
    - BOOT COMPLETED (OS Boot, sensor type 0x1F, offset 0x06), when the status read on the BMC service's
      supervisor channel (above) shows every managed service in a healthy end state - the test behind
      ServiceManager's `online` report, `all_settled`, which accepts Ready and Stopped. The answer spells
      those two `running` and `stopped`, and it carries more than the managed services: after them come the
      canary's row and one row per manifest driver, and those carry NO wanted state (a driver not bound
      reads `pending` for the whole boot). So the condition is that EVERY ROW WITH A WANTED STATE reads
      `running` or `stopped`, and the rows without one are not read. At most once per boot: an instance
      whose own row in that status shows a restart does not write it again;
    - AN ORDERLY SHUTDOWN (OS Stop/Shutdown, sensor type 0x20, offset 0x03, for a power-off and a reboot
      alike), riding P02M0200's orderly-shutdown notice - the bounded step inside ServiceManager's ONE orderly
      sequence, which the shell's `shutdown` and `reboot` and P02M0198d's `system-shutdown` all reach. The
      BMC service's manifest row declares the notice; on `prepare` it asks the driver, which sends the event,
      and it answers once the event is sent or the notice's bound is spent. No second notice is defined here.
      IMMEDIATE `system-power` CALLS - the Power key, Ctrl+Alt+Delete, DeviceManager's power-button path,
      P02M0198d's `power-off-within` - carry no notice by design, so they log no shutdown event; nor does a
      BMC hard stop.
  A kernel panic writes nothing: see EXCLUDES.
- [ ] Chassis status, and the identify light (on for 1 to 255 seconds, or off), in the tool's scope. POWER
  CONTROL - power down, power cycle, hard reset, soft shutdown - is an administrative action below, and IT IS
  NOT A SECOND DOOR TO THE SYSTEM'S OWN STOP. `system-power` stays the only way the system stops ITSELF, and
  the shell's `shutdown` and `reboot` its orderly form; a chassis operation is the BMC acting on the hardware
  from outside - which is why it exists (a device a reboot does not clear, a power cycle) and why it needs a
  person. Power down, power cycle and hard reset are HARD STOPS, and the protected screen says so; soft
  shutdown reaches the system as its power button and takes the system's own power-button path - today
  DeviceManager's immediate power-off, later whatever P02M0197's button policy makes it. Power up and the
  diagnostic interrupt are refused at preparation.
- [ ] THE BMC WATCHDOG AS A `watchdog` PROVIDER for P02M0200, published by the driver's KCS, BT and PCI forms
  and never by SSIF (P02M0201a): Set Watchdog Timer (one stage, timer use "OS", action hard reset, no
  pre-timeout, "don't stop" set) and Reset Watchdog Timer to arm; Reset Watchdog Timer to pet, at the head of
  the queue; Set Watchdog Timer with "don't stop" clear to disarm, which the BMC allows; a timeout range of
  15 s to 6553.5 s in 100 ms steps; no pre-timeout warning; SURVIVES the platform's reset and keeps counting
  in a sleep; "the last reset was the watchdog's" from Get Watchdog Timer's expiration flags, read at bind and
  then cleared; a timer found running at bind (the firmware's or the last boot's) petted at once and taken
  over, as P02M0200 requires, the bind line naming the initial countdown Get Watchdog Timer answered, which
  is P02M0200c's oracle for the shutdown notice.
- [ ] LAN configuration and users READ-ONLY, in the tool's scope: per LAN channel the address source,
  address, mask, gateway, MAC and VLAN; per channel each user's id, name, enabled state and privilege limit.
  Changing either is excluded.
- [ ] THE ADMINSERVICE EXTENSION, because two operations here erase the log or stop the machine:
    - `liber:admin@1`'s `admin-action` gains `bmc-sel-clear = 3` and `bmc-chassis-control = 4`, appended at
      version 1, and `admin_descriptor::Action` gains both, with their names and wire values;
    - TWO executor publications, `org.libersystem.admin-bmc-sel` and `org.libersystem.admin-bmc-chassis`,
      each mapped to ONE action in AdminService's executor table - so its one-name-one-action rule, and its
      refusal of a second executor for one action, stay as they are;
    - ONE BMC PER MACHINE IS ADMINISTRABLE, which follows from that rule: AdminService keeps the first
      executor published for each action and refuses every later one, so both actions reach the BMC whose
      `ipmi` binding published first - for a pair, the same BMC either way - and a request naming another BMC
      is refused by that executor at preparation, and declined;
    - PermissionManager's `admin_policy` gains the row `bmc`: those two actions, on targets starting `bmc:`,
      which the request scope's bound of two holds; no other component gets it;
    - WHAT A REQUEST CARRIES, inside the landed path's three rules - AdminService declines a request without a
      payload, the executor seam refuses an empty one, and a preparation whose parameters differ from the
      request's is declined as another operation: the target is `bmc:` and the BMC's identity; the parameters
      are, for SEL clear, the entry count the `bmc` tool read through the BMC service (two bytes), and for
      chassis control the operation (one byte: 0 power down, 2 power cycle, 3 hard reset, 5 soft shutdown);
      the payload is the target selector's own bytes - fixed, saying nothing the target does not, refused by
      the executor if it is anything else, and shown by its length and digest as every payload is. The
      executor freezes the target at the binding's generation, and revalidation fails once the binding or the
      BMC changed;
    - SEL CLEAR ERASES ONLY WHAT WAS COUNTED, by IPMI's reserve-check-clear: at preparation the executor
      refuses a count that is not the live one (Get SEL Info) and takes Reserve SEL; at execution it sends
      Clear SEL under THAT reservation, which the BMC cancels when an event is added in between, so the clear
      fails, nothing is erased and the outcome is `failed`. On a BMC whose Get SEL Info reports no Reserve SEL
      the clear is refused at preparation. The erasure is then polled to completion within the deadline
      through Get SEL Info, which answers 0x81 while an erase is in progress and takes no reservation (the
      clear cancelled its own);
    - THE SCREEN SAYS WHAT IT DOES, in AdminService's words: `admin_descriptor::prompt` gains an `Operation:`
      line rendered from the action and its parameters by a fixed table - "erase all N records of the BMC's
      event log; an event that arrives first cancels it"; "HARD RESET", "POWER DOWN" or "POWER CYCLE" with
      "the machine stops at once: no service is stopped and nothing is flushed"; "SOFT SHUTDOWN: the BMC
      presses the power button" - each followed by "the payload is the target's name", and the descriptor
      check refuses parameters the table cannot render;
    - the journal records them as it records every action - the descriptor's digest, and no secret, since
      neither action carries one;
    - host tests for the rendering, the descriptor checks and the executor's count, payload and reservation
      rules; the gate cases in P02M0201c.
- [ ] `bmc`, a shipping tool: `info`, `sensors`, `sel` (and `--follow`), `sel clear` (asking with the entry
  count it has just read), `fru`, `chassis` (status, `identify`, and `power-down`, `power-cycle`,
  `hard-reset` and `soft-shutdown` as requests), `lan` and `users`, each naming the BMC by its identity. A BMC
  that is unavailable or refuses is said to be, with its completion code. Its PermissionManager row, as
  `btctl` and `dfu` have theirs: a new `Bmc` capability for the BMC service's root, and `AdminRequest` under
  the `bmc` scope.
